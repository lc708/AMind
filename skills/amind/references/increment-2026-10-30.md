## New evidence and its limits

- **[Public position] Architecture:** [When to fold subagents into your main agent loop](https://claude.com/resources/articles/building-agents-when-to-fold-subagents-into-your-main-agent-loop)
  (October 22) gives conditions for merging tightly coupled routing/planning/domain
  roles. Independent validation, parallel work and permission isolation can still
  justify delegation. Do not generalize this into an unconditional single-agent rule.
- **[Public position] Human judgment:** [How AI agents shape experts' judgment calls](https://www.anthropic.com/research/agents-judgment-calls)
  (October 27) measures effects of experimentally assigned agent preferences in
  one analyst task. The 86% following rate is not an error rate. Approval and
  review do not alone establish independent judgment.
- **[Public position] Oversight:** [Red-Teaming Auto Mode](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/red-teaming-auto-mode/)
  (October 22) studies September monitor snapshots, including multi-context and
  compaction attacks. It does not certify current products or test their sandbox.
  The [UK AISI assessment](https://www.anthropic.com/research/alignment-assessment-ukaisi)
  (October 23) analyzes an earlier Mythos 5 incident; publication is not the event date.
- **[Public position] Software security:** [Attack-surface reduction](https://www.anthropic.com/research/attack-surface-reduction),
  [formal verification](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formal-verification) and
  [vulnerability chains](https://www.anthropic.com/research/discovering-vulnerabilities-with-claude)
  add prevention and bounded verification methods. Issues, validated vulnerabilities,
  proven entry points and model exploit results are different populations.
- **[Public position] Training:** [GRAFT](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/graft/)
  (October 19) is a named collaborative small-model study, not a claim that Anthropic
  has deployed that training method generally.
- **[Public position] Science:** [Scorpion-toxin binders](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-scorpion-toxin-binder-design)
  and [drug-development work](https://www.anthropic.com/research/early-progress-drug-development)
  retain human/lab contributions and preclinical limits. Binding does not demonstrate
  toxin neutralization or patient benefit.
- **[Public position] Interpretability:** [October Circuits Updates](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/october-update/index.html)
  presents preliminary J++-lens work. The body gives a month and the feed an update
  timestamp; neither supplies an invented exact publication day.
