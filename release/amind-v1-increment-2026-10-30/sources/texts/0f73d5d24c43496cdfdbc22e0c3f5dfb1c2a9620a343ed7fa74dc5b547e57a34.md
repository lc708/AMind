Query: "What theorem did White prove about the injectivity radius of closed hyperbolic 3-manifolds in terms of the rank of their fundamental group?"

Answer: "White proved that for all r there is R such that every closed hyperbolic 3-manifold M with rank(π1(M)) ≤r has inj(M) ≤R. In other words, the injectivity radius of a closed hyperbolic 3-manifold is bounded above in terms of the rank of its fundamental group."
