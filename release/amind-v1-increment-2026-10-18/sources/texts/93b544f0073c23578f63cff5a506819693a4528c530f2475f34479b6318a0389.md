Our Usage Policy (also referred to as our “Acceptable Use Policy” or “AUP”) applies to anyone who submits inputs to Anthropic’s products or services, all of whom we refer to as “users.” This includes individuals using our apps (such as Claude.ai and Claude Code), developers and businesses using our API and developer platforms, customers accessing Claude through cloud providers and authorized resellers, and the end users of products or services integrating Claude. The Usage Policy is intended to prevent our products and services from being used to create or contribute to real-world harm, and to promote the responsible use of our products and services.

Please review this Usage Policy carefully before using our products or services.

We will update our policy as our technology and the associated risks evolve or as we learn about unanticipated risks.

Our Usage Policy is divided into three categories:

- Universal Usage Standards: Our Universal Usage Standards are prohibitions that apply to all users and use cases.

- High-risk Use Case Requirements: Our High-risk Use Case Requirements apply to specific use cases that pose an elevated risk of harm to individuals.

- Additional Use Case Guidelines: Our Additional Use Case Guidelines apply to certain other use cases, including consumer-facing chatbots and agents, products serving minors, agentic use, and software listed in our directories.

Our Usage Policy focuses on how our products and services are used. Its prohibitions and requirements do not categorically prohibit use by any particular industry or line of business, provided the use of our products and services complies with this Usage Policy.

Anthropic’s Safeguards Team implements detection and monitoring to enforce our Usage Policy. If we suspect that you may have violated our Usage Policy, we may warn you or throttle, limit, suspend, or terminate your access to our products and services.

Our products also include real-time safeguards that may block or limit certain outputs, but encountering a block does not by itself indicate a violation of the Usage Policy. For certain dual-use or high-risk use cases covered by real-time safeguards in select domains, we may offer programs where you can apply to be granted access to our products and services with adjusted safeguards, such as our Cyber Verification Program and Life Sciences Verification Program, subject to program-specific eligibility criteria.

If you believe that our model outputs are potentially inaccurate, biased, or harmful, please notify us at usersafety@anthropic.com, or report it directly in our product through the “report issues” thumbs down button or similar feedback features (where available). You can read more about our Safeguards practices and recommendations in our Safeguards Support Center.

This Usage Policy is calibrated to strike an optimal balance between enabling beneficial uses and mitigating potential harms. Anthropic may enter into contracts with certain governmental customers that tailor use restrictions to that customer’s public mission and legal authorities if, in Anthropic’s judgment, the contractual use restrictions and applicable safeguards are adequate to mitigate the potential harms addressed by this Usage Policy.

## Do Not Violate Applicable Laws or Engage in Illegal Activity

This includes using our products or services to:

- Illegally produce, acquire, sell, or distribute controlled or regulated substances

- Engage in or facilitate human trafficking or prostitution

- Infringe, misappropriate, or violate the intellectual property rights of a third party

- Violate any other applicable laws or regulations in your jurisdiction

## Do Not Compromise Critical Infrastructure

This includes using our products or services to:

- Facilitate the destruction or disruption of critical infrastructure such as power grids, water treatment facilities, medical devices, telecommunication networks, emergency services, or transportation systems

- Obtain unauthorized access to critical systems such as election, healthcare, or financial systems

- Interfere with the operation of military bases and related infrastructure

## Do Not Compromise Computer or Network Systems Without Authorization

This includes using our products or services to:

- Discover or exploit vulnerabilities in systems, networks, or applications without authorization of the system owner

- Gain unauthorized access to, or escalate unauthorized privileges within, systems, networks, applications, or devices through technical attacks or social engineering

- Create or install tools designed to intercept communications or monitor devices without authorization of the system owner

- Develop persistent unauthorized remote access tools designed to operate below normal system security levels, including firmware modifications or hardware implants

- Create automated tools designed to compromise multiple systems at scale without authorization

- Bypass security controls such as authentication systems, endpoint protection, or monitoring tools without authorization

- Create or distribute malware, ransomware, or other types of malicious code

- Conduct denial-of-service attacks, or develop tools for denial-of-service attacks or managing botnets

This section does not prohibit security research, testing, or tool development conducted on systems you own or operate, with the authorization of the system owner, or within the scope of an authorized bug bounty program or vulnerability disclosure policy, provided the activity complies with applicable law. For work covered by our real-time cyber safeguards, users may apply for adjusted access through our Cyber Verification Program.

- Facilitate the trafficking, sextortion, or any other form of exploitation of a minor

- Facilitate child abuse of any form, including instructions for how to conceal abuse

Any specific age eligibility requirements for use of our products will be set out in our Terms of Service.

## Do Not Use for Surveillance, Censorship, or Prohibited Law Enforcement or Criminal Justice Purposes

This includes using our products or services to:

- Target or track a person or their property without that person’s consent by monitoring their physical location, biometrics, movements, online activities, communications, or associations, whether in real time, on an ongoing basis, or through analysis of previously collected data. This includes using our products for facial recognition, battlefield management, predictive policing, or monitoring in law enforcement investigations.

- Build, improve, or enhance the surveillance capabilities of tools, systems, or infrastructure designed for the surveillance uses prohibited in this section

- Identify, unmask, or locate people from anonymous or pseudonymous activity, or compile dossiers or aggregate personal information on individuals, in order to harass, intimidate, surveil, or cause harm to the target (e.g., doxxing)

- Utilize models to assign scores or ratings to individuals based on an assessment of their trustworthiness or social behavior without notification or their consent

- Build or support emotional recognition systems or techniques that are used to infer emotions of a natural person, except for medical or safety reasons

This section does not prohibit analysis or tracking that individuals have agreed to, such as for fraud detection or anti-money laundering screening on a financial account, or consumer apps where a user has opted in. It also does not prohibit aggregated or anonymized analysis, content moderation, authorized security research, legal research and analysis by law enforcement agencies or courts, or journalism, provided these activities are not used for the purposes prohibited above.
